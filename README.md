# 🚀 CollabTasks

**Streamline your projects, synchronize your team.**

CollabTasks is a comprehensive productivity workspace that seamlessly integrates advanced task management with real-time communication. From tracking corporate deadlines with multimedia attachments to organizing family routines, it empowers you to coordinate every project and team member within one intuitive ecosystem.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 📋 Smart Task Management
* **Project Boards:** Organize tasks into distinct projects with clear progress tracking.
* **Deadline Tracking:** Never miss a milestone with visual reminders and status updates.
* **Team Assignments:** See exactly who is doing what at any given moment.
* **Multimedia Integration:** Attach files, photos, and videos directly to tasks for full context.

### 💬 Unified Communication
* **Instant Messaging:** Personal and group chats to keep discussions focused.
* **High-Quality Calls:** Built-in audio and video calls to resolve issues instantly without leaving the app.
* **Real-time Sync:** All updates and messages are synced across all participants immediately.

## 🛠 Tech Stack
* **Framework & Language:** Flutter & Dart for cross-platform performance.
* **Architecture:** Clean Architecture (Separation of Domain, Data, and Presentation layers).
* **Local Database:** Drift (Moor) or Hive for efficient local data persistence.
* **Dependency Injection:** GetIt & Injectable for modular and testable code.
* **Rich Text Editing:** Integrated `flutter_quill` for advanced multimedia task descriptions.
* **Communication:** WebRTC / Agora for high-quality audio and video calls.
